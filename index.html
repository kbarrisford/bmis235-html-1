The Florida Everglades

A page about an important ecosystem

The Everglades is a vast wetland in southern Florida and one of the most unique ecosystems in the world. It is home to hundreds of species of plants and animals, including alligators, manatees, and the endangered Florida panther.

The Everglades covers approximately 1.5 million acres. Often called the “River of Grass,” it is a slow-moving freshwater ecosystem that flows from Lake Okeechobee toward Florida Bay and the Gulf of Mexico.
